Leapcure World Health Day 2025: Healthy Beginnings, Hopeful Futures World Health Day, celebrated on April 7th, promotes global health awareness and equitable healthcare. The 2025 theme, "Healthy Beginnings, Hopeful Futures," focuses on improving maternal and newborn health, advocating for access to quality care and reducing preventable deaths. It emphasizes health equity and encourages community involvement and policy change for better health outcomes.
