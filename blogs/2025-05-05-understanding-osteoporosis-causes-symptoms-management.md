Leapcure Understanding Osteoporosis: Causes, Symptoms & Management Osteoporosis is a chronic condition characterized by reduced bone density, leading to fragile bones and increased fracture risk, particularly in postmenopausal women and older adults. Early detection, screening, and proactive management with lifestyle changes, supplements, and medications can help maintain bone health, prevent fractures, and improve quality of life.
