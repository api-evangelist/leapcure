Leapcure Prader-Willi Syndrome Information and Resources: Causes, Insights, and Treatment Options Prader-Willi Syndrome (PWS) is a rare genetic disorder characterized by hyperphagia—an insatiable hunger that can lead to life-threatening obesity. This comprehensive guide explores the causes, symptoms, and management strategies for PWS, including approaches to address hyperphagia through structured dietary control and behavioral interventions. Discover expert resources and support networks for families navigating the unique challenges of Prader-Willi Syndrome.
