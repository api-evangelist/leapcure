Leapcure Frequently Asked Questions About Hereditary Angioedema (HAE) Hereditary Angioedema (HAE) is a rare and often misunderstood genetic disorder. It can cause recurrent episodes of severe swelling in different parts of the body, including the skin, gastrointestinal tract, and airways. Here, we address some of the most frequently asked questions about HAE to help patients, families, and caregivers better understand this condition.
