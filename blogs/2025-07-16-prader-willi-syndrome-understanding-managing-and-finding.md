Leapcure Prader-Willi Syndrome: Understanding, Managing, and Finding Hope Prader-Willi syndrome (PWS) is a rare genetic disorder affecting 1 in 15,000 to 20,000 individuals, causing various physical and mental challenges. Symptoms include hypotonia in infancy and hyperphagia later. Although there's no cure, early diagnosis and multidisciplinary management can improve quality of life.
