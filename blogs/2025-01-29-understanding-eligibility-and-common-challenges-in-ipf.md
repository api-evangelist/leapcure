Leapcure Understanding Eligibility and Common Challenges in IPF Clinical Trials Eligibility criteria in IPF clinical trials ensure participant safety and reliable results. Common reasons for disqualification include medication use, exclusionary medical conditions, and lack of a confirmed diagnosis. Understanding these criteria helps patients navigate the trial process and explore available options to improve their outcomes and manage expectations.
