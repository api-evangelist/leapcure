Leapcure Rethinking Rare Disease Clinical Trial Recruitment: When the Data Doesn’t Tell the Truth In rare disease clinical trials, data misinterpretation can lead to false positives despite promising metrics. A recent study revealed that while patients passed initial screenings, many didn't meet strict eligibility criteria. By refining the screener to better align with patient language and experiences, recruitment improved quality, highlighting the importance of clear screening processes and human insight.
